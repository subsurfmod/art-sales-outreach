# High-Converting Physical Art Email Playbook

## Why Most Cold Art Emails Get Ignored (and How to Fix It)

| Common Mistake | Why It Fails | High-Reply Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Attaching 5 large JPEGs or a 10MB PDF on Email #1 | Triggers spam filters & bounces; feels pushy | Send **plain-text** Email #1 with **1 clean portfolio URL** and ask permission: *"Open to me sending a 5-piece private catalog PDF?"* |
| Talking only about your artist statement / childhood | Buyers care about *their client's wall*, scale, palette, and reliability | Lead with **their aesthetic/project** (1 sentence), then state scale, medium, trade terms, and reliability |
| Generic "Dear Sir/Madam, I am an artist selling paintings" | Instantly deleted as spam | Use enriched company name, first name (when found), and real aesthetic/project hook |
| Sending only 1 email and giving up | Busy designers & curators miss Email #1; **~65–75% of replies happen on Touch 2 or 3** | Run a polite 3-touch sequence (Day 0 → Day +4 → Day +10) and stop immediately on any reply |

---

## The 4-Part Cold Art Email Formula (75–120 Words Max)

1. **Grounded Opener (1 sentence)**: Prove you actually looked at their work (reference their city, style tags, or a specific project).
2. **Concrete Visual Positioning (1–2 sentences)**: Medium + palette/mood + physical dimensions (`36×48" to 60×84"`). Buyers cannot picture your work without scale and medium.
3. **Role-Specific Value Prop (1 sentence)**:
   - *Interior Designers*: Trade pricing (15–25%) + custom sizing + **free digital wall mockup**.
   - *Art Consultants*: Ready-to-ship studio inventory + reliable commission turnaround + crated shipping.
   - *Luxury Stagers*: Consignment/placement option + referral commission when the homebuyer buys the art.
   - *Hospitality*: Cohesive multi-piece series tailored to the property palette.
   - *Galleries*: Curatorial fit + brief link (no salesy hype, no "trade discount" language!).
4. **Low-Friction CTA (1 question)**: Ask for a tiny "yes", never a 30-minute call on Email #1.
   - *"Open to me sending a 5-piece trade catalog PDF?"*
   - *"Have a blank wall on an active project I could mock up digitally for you?"*

## The "Free Room Mockup" Super-Weapon

Interior designers and hospitality buyers hesitate to buy physical art online because they can't judge scale and color in situ. Offering:
> *"Send me a quick phone photo of any wall you're styling and I'll drop 2–3 pieces onto it to scale at no charge"*

removes 100% of their risk, turns a cold pitch into a helpful creative collaboration, and converts at **3×–5× higher rates** than a generic "check out my shop" link.

## Subject Line Rules (High Open Rate, Zero Spam Vibe)

- Keep subject lines **3–6 words**, lowercase or sentence-case, sounding like a peer wrote it.
- Good examples:
  - `Original oil paintings for {{company}} projects?`
  - `Artist roster — {{artist_name}}`
  - `Statement art for {{company}} stagings`
  - `Quick question about {{company}}'s art sourcing`
  - `{{medium}} for {{location}} interiors`
- **Avoid spam triggers**: `FREE!!!`, `ACT NOW`, `LIMITED TIME`, `BUY NOW`, `100% GUARANTEED`, ALL CAPS, excessive emojis, or exclamation marks.

## Converting a Positive Reply Into a Physical Sale

When `art-outreach outreach check-replies` flags a warm reply:
1. **Reply within 2 hours** (speed dramatically increases close rate).
2. Send a **tightly curated 4–6 piece PDF lookbook** (under 3 MB, high-res images + dimensions + retail & trade pricing + shipping lead time). Never dump 60 random pieces.
3. Offer **1 concrete next step**:
   - Digital wall mockup on their project photo, OR
   - Studio video walkthrough (60-second phone video showing texture, depth, and edges in natural light — physical art buyers LOVE seeing texture in motion!), OR
   - Local studio visit / short-term on-approval placement (for vetted local designers/collectors).
